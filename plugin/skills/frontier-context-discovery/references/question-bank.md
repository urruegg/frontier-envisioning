# Discovery question bank

Six categories, used in the context pack (`open_questions[].category`) and in the blueprint's Discovery view. Pick the questions the evidence has not answered yet; never ask what a source already answered.

## Core questions (any journey)

| Category | Questions |
|---|---|
| Business & process | Which steps are rule-based and which need judgement? Who decides at each step, on which thresholds? What volumes run through the journey per month? Where do work items wait the longest? |
| Data | Where does each step's data live, and is it complete and current? Which data is confidential or personal? Which data products exist on the data platform? |
| Systems & integration | Which systems of record does the journey touch? Do they expose APIs or connectors? Which actions may a workflow perform with a service identity? Which integration platform is already in place? |
| Governance & risk | What may an agent decide alone, and what must a person approve? Which regulations apply (revFADP, GDPR, EU AI Act, sector rules)? Is works-council approval needed? Where must data reside? Is real data available under NDA? |
| Value & KPIs | Which KPI would prove success, and what is the baseline? What is one hour, one day or one percent worth? Who owns the KPI? |
| Value inputs | How many cases per month? How long does one take today, end to end? What does one hour cost, loaded? What does an error, a second visit or a late case cost? Which spend would stop (overtime, external service, a hire)? |
| Cost & FinOps | Who holds Microsoft 365 Copilot today and next year? EA, MCA or CSP; MACC; existing capacity packs or P3? Billing currency and price sheet? Who owns the agent budget, and how are costs charged back? Budget cycle? |
| Adoption & change | Which team would co-design and pilot first? How will roles, targets and incentives change? Who becomes the agent boss? |

## Journey-specific starters

| Journey | Questions that usually decide the hypothesis |
|---|---|
| Source-to-Pay (procurement) | Share of spend through catalogues and frame contracts? Tail-spend volume and supplier count? Delegation-of-authority limits? Requisition API in the S2P suite? Contract repository and clause playbook? |
| Lead-to-Order (sales) | Lead volume and channels? Quote cycle time and error rate? Configurator and price-book ownership? Discount approval thresholds? CRM data quality? |
| Request-to-Resolution (service) | Visits per month and second-visit rate? Report completion time? Telemetry availability? Field service system actions for a service identity? Works council on voice and location data? |
| Record-to-Report (finance) | Days to close and the critical path? Unmatched reconciliation items? Commentary process? Internal controls an agent must satisfy? |
| Hire-to-Retire (HR) | System of record for employee data? Which decisions about people must stay human, and who is named? Document intake volumes? Data classification of HR data? |
| Plan-to-Deliver (supply chain) | Planning cycle and forecast accuracy? Early-warning signals available? Supplier collaboration portal? |
| Incident-to-Resolve (IT service) | Ticket volume by category? Knowledge base quality? Automation already in the ITSM tool? |
| Customer service | Contact volume by channel? First-contact resolution? Authentication and consent for customer data? |

## Frontier operating-model questions (for the leadership session)

- Which processes should be human-led but agent-operated within 18 months?
- Who is the agent boss for each lead agent, and how many agents can one person supervise well (the human-agent ratio)?
- Who runs the agent lifecycle (Intelligence Resources: IT and HR together)?
- How are agent outcomes reviewed, and who may change an agent's instructions?
- Which collaboration pattern fits each step: author, editor, director or orchestrator?
